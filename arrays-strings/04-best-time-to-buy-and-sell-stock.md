## Problem: Best Time to Buy and Sell Stock (Easy–Medium)

**Link:** [Best Time to Buy and Sell Stock](https://leetcode.com/problems/best-time-to-buy-and-sell-stock/)

### Approach
Keep the minimum price seen so far and calculate the best profit at every price.

### Complexity
- Time: O(n)
- Space: O(1)

### Notes
Continuously decreasing prices give 0 profit.
